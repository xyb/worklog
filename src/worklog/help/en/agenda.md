---
title: agenda — everything scheduled in a date range
category: command
see_also: sched, day, planning
---
`wl agenda <start> <end>` lists everything scheduled in `[start, end]` — across day / week /
month / quarter granularity in one unified view, for "what's on my plate this stretch?".

  wl agenda 2026-06-01 2026-06-30

Use it when planning a week or month so you don't double-book or miss an already-scheduled
task. Scheduling is `wl sched` (see `wl help sched`); a single day's detail is `wl day`.
